# AVESTELLAR FINANCE - FINANCIAL METRICS & CALCULATION ENGINE CATALOG

## 1. Overview and Core Invariants

The AVESTELLAR FINANCE Calculation Engine is a deterministic, high-precision financial metrics engine built upon double-entry posted accounting records.

### Core Architecture Invariants:
1. **Zero Binary Floating-Point Arithmetic**: All monetary amounts, interest rates, margins, and ratios are calculated using Python's `Decimal` with explicit quantization (`0.0001` for currency amounts, `0.01` for percentages).
2. **Double-Entry Ledger as Ground Truth**: Metrics derive exclusively from posted journal entries (`JournalStatus.POSTED`). Draft or unposted transactions are excluded from historical calculations.
3. **Immutability and Isolation**: Simulations and scenarios never write to or modify the official general ledger. Multi-tenant isolation is enforced via organizational scoping and headers (`X-Organization-ID`).
4. **Explicit Statuses over Zero Fabrication**: When required inputs are missing or denominators are zero, the engine returns explicit status codes (`available`, `insufficient_data`, `undefined`, `not_applicable`, `unsupported_currency`) rather than reporting a fabricated zero.

## 2. Metric Specifications

### A. Profitability & Income Statement Metrics

#### 1. Gross Profit & Gross Profit Margin
- **Identifier**: `gross_profit`, `gross_profit_margin`
- **Formula**:
  - $\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Revenue} - \text{COGS}$
  - $\text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Currency (Base) / Percentage (`%`)
- **Required Accounts**:
  - Revenue accounts (`category=REVENUE` or mapped `FinancialClassification.REVENUE`)
  - Contra-revenue accounts (`FinancialClassification.CONTRA_REVENUE`)
  - Cost of Goods Sold accounts (`FinancialClassification.COGS`)
- **Period**: Reporting date interval `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Derivation**: Ledger-derived from posted journals.
- **Interpretation**: Measures product-level direct profitability before operating overhead and financing costs.
- **Limitations**: Undefined when net revenue is zero or negative.
- **Worked Example**:
  - Net Revenue = KES 1,000,000.00
  - COGS = KES 400,000.00
  - Gross Profit = KES 600,000.00
  - Gross Margin = $\frac{600,000}{1,000,000} \times 100 = 60.00\%$
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/profitability/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_profitability_and_ebitda_bridge`

#### 2. Operating Expenses & OPEX Ratio
- **Identifier**: `operating_expenses`, `opex_ratio`
- **Formula**:
  - $\text{OPEX} = \sum \text{Operating Expense Balances (Excluding COGS, Interest, Taxes, Depr/Amort)}$
  - $\text{OPEX Ratio \%} = \frac{\text{OPEX}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Currency (Base) / Percentage (`%`)
- **Required Accounts**: Mapped `FinancialClassification.OPEX_GENERAL` or `OPEX_ADMIN`
- **Period**: `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Derivation**: Ledger-derived.
- **Interpretation**: Evaluates organizational overhead efficiency relative to sales.
- **Limitations**: Does not distinguish fixed from variable costs without explicit cost behavior mappings.
- **Worked Example**:
  - OPEX = KES 200,000.00
  - Net Revenue = KES 1,000,000.00
  - OPEX Ratio = $20.00\%$
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/profitability/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_profitability_and_ebitda_bridge`

#### 3. EBIT & Operating Margin
- **Identifier**: `ebit`, `ebit_margin`
- **Formula**:
  - $\text{EBIT} = \text{Net Revenue} - \text{COGS} - \text{OPEX} - \text{Depreciation} - \text{Amortization}$
  - $\text{EBIT Margin \%} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Currency (Base) / Percentage (`%`)
- **Required Accounts**: Revenue, COGS, OPEX, Depreciation, Amortization
- **Period**: `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Derivation**: Ledger-derived.
- **Interpretation**: Measures core operational profitability independent of capital structure and tax jurisdictions.
- **Limitations**: Not equivalent to cash generated by operations.
- **Worked Example**:
  - Gross Profit = KES 600,000.00, OPEX = KES 200,000.00, Depreciation = KES 50,000.00
  - EBIT = $600,000 - 200,000 - 50,000 = \text{KES } 350,000.00$
  - EBIT Margin = $35.00\%$
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/profitability/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_profitability_and_ebitda_bridge`

#### 4. EBITDA & Formal Reconciliation Bridge
- **Identifier**: `ebitda`, `ebitda_margin`
- **Formula**:
  - $\text{EBITDA} = \text{Net Income} + \text{Interest Expense} - \text{Interest Income} + \text{Income Tax} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Amortization}$
  - $\text{EBITDA Margin \%} = \frac{\text{EBITDA}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Currency (Base) / Percentage (`%`)
- **Required Accounts**: Full income statement classifications
- **Period**: `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Derivation**: Ledger-derived with verified mathematical bridge.
- **Reconciliation Bridge**:
  ```
  Net Income (KES 300,000.00)
  + Interest Expense (KES 30,000.00)
  + Income Tax Expense (KES 20,000.00)
  + Depreciation Expense (KES 50,000.00)
  + Amortization Expense (KES 0.00)
  = EBITDA (KES 400,000.00)
  ```
- **Worked Example**:
  - EBITDA = KES 400,000.00; Net Revenue = KES 1,000,000.00
  - EBITDA Margin = $40.00\%$
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/profitability/`
- **AI Explanation**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/ai/explain-ebitda/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_profitability_and_ebitda_bridge`

### B. Capital Allocation & Return Metrics

#### 5. Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) & CAPEX Ratios
- **Identifier**: `capex`, `capex_to_revenue`, `capex_to_depreciation`
- **Formula**:
  - $\text{CAPEX} = \sum \text{Incurred Capital Expenditures} + \Delta \text{PPE Asset Additions}$
  - $\text{CAPEX to Revenue \%} = \frac{\text{CAPEX}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100$
  - $\text{CAPEX to Depreciation \%} = \frac{\text{CAPEX}}{\text{Depreciation}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Currency / Percentage (`%`)
- **Required Inputs**: `CapitalExpenditure` records or fixed asset account movements
- **Period**: `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Interpretation**: Ratios $>100\%$ relative to depreciation indicate expansionary capital reinvestment; $<100\%$ indicates net asset contraction.
- **Worked Example**:
  - Incurred CAPEX = KES 150,000.00; Depreciation = KES 50,000.00
  - CAPEX to Depreciation = $300.00\%$
- **API Route**: `GET|POST /api/v1/analytics/capital-expenditure/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_capex_and_cost_behavior`

#### 6. Return on Assets (ROA)
- **Identifier**: `roa`
- **Formula**: $\text{ROA \%} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Average Total Assets}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Percentage (`%`)
- **Period**: `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Interpretation**: Evaluates efficiency of total asset deployment in generating bottom-line earnings.
- **Limitations**: Undefined when average asset base is zero or negative.
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/returns/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_return_metrics_roa_roe_roic_roce`

#### 7. Return on Equity (ROE)
- **Identifier**: `roe`
- **Formula**: $\text{ROE \%} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Average Total Equity}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Percentage (`%`)
- **Period**: `[start_date, end_date]`
- **Safety Invariant**: Returns status `undefined` when average equity is zero or negative, preventing fabricated positive returns for technically insolvent entities.
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/returns/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_negative_or_zero_equity_roe_handling`

#### 8. Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) & Capital Employed (ROCE)
- **Identifier**: `roic`, `roce`
- **Formula**:
  - $\text{ROIC \%} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Average Invested Capital}} \times 100$
    - $\text{NOPAT} = \text{EBIT} \times (1 - \text{Effective Tax Rate})$
    - $\text{Invested Capital} = \text{Total Debt} + \text{Equity} - \text{Excess Cash}$
  - $\text{ROCE \%} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Total Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities}} \times 100$
- **Units**: Percentage (`%`)
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/returns/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_return_metrics_roa_roe_roic_roce`

### C. Investment Appraisal Suite (Deterministic Calculators)

#### 9. Net Present Value (NPV)
- **Identifier**: `npv`
- **Formula**: $\text{NPV} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{CF_t}{(1 + r)^t} - C_0$
- **Units**: Currency (Decimal)
- **Inputs**:
  - `initial_investment`: Non-negative decimal ($C_0$)
  - `cash_flows`: Sequence of net periodic inflows ($CF_t$)
  - `discount_rate`: Periodic discount rate ($r > -1.0$)
- **Worked Example**:
  - $C_0 = 100.00$, $CF_1 = 110.00$, $r = 0.10$
  - $\text{NPV} = \frac{110.00}{1.10} - 100.00 = 0.0000$
- **API Route**: `POST /api/v1/analytics/calculators/npv/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_investment_npv_and_irr`

#### 10. Internal Rate of Return (IRR) - Hybrid Numerical Solver
- **Identifier**: `irr`
- **Formula**: $r$ such that $\text{NPV}(r) = 0$
- **Units**: Percentage (`%`)
- **Algorithm**:
  - Hybrid Newton-Raphson with analytical derivative:
    $$f'(r) = -\sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{t \cdot CF_t}{(1 + r)^{t+1}}$$
  - Automatic fallback to Bisection when derivative approaches zero or step escapes bounds $[-0.9999, 10.0]$.
  - Descartes' Rule of Signs check: Identifies multi-root ambiguities when sign changes $> 1$.
  - Returns `undefined` or `not_applicable` if all flows are negative (no economic solution exists).
- **Worked Example**:
  - $C_0 = 100.00$, $CF_1 = 110.00 \implies \text{IRR} = 10.0000\%$
- **API Route**: `POST /api/v1/analytics/calculators/irr/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_irr_no_solution_and_ambiguous_cases`

#### 11. Simple & Discounted Payback Period
- **Identifier**: `payback_period`
- **Formula**: Period $t$ where cumulative cash flows equal initial investment, with linear fractional interpolation within the recovery period.
- **Units**: Periods / Years
- **API Route**: `POST /api/v1/analytics/calculators/investment/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_payback_periods_and_profitability_index`

#### 12. Profitability Index (PI)
- **Identifier**: `profitability_index`
- **Formula**: $\text{PI} = \frac{\text{PV of Future Cash Flows}}{C_0}$
- **Units**: Ratio ($\text{x}$)
- **Decision Rule**: Accept proposal if $\text{PI} > 1.0$.
- **API Route**: `POST /api/v1/analytics/calculators/investment/`

### D. Liquidity, Solvency & Leverage Ratios

#### 13. Working Capital & Liquidity Ratios
- **Identifiers**: `working_capital`, `current_ratio`, `quick_ratio`, `cash_ratio`, `cash_runway`
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Working Capital} = \text{Current Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities}$
  - $\text{Current Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$
  - $\text{Quick Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets} - \text{Inventory}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$
  - $\text{Cash Ratio} = \frac{\text{Cash \& Equivalents}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$
  - $\text{Cash Runway (Months)} = \frac{\text{Verified Liquid Cash}}{\text{Monthly Burn Rate}}$
- **Safety Invariant**: Returns `undefined` when current liabilities are zero.
- **Worked Example**:
  - Current Assets = KES 500,000.00; Current Liabilities = KES 250,000.00; Inventory = KES 100,000.00; Cash = KES 300,000.00
  - Working Capital = KES 250,000.00
  - Current Ratio = $2.00\text{x}$
  - Quick Ratio = $\frac{400,000}{250,000} = 1.60\text{x}$
  - Cash Ratio = $\frac{300,000}{250,000} = 1.20\text{x}$
  - Cash Runway (KES 50,000 burn) = $\frac{300,000}{50,000} = 6.0\text{ months}$
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/liquidity-leverage/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_liquidity_ratios_and_zero_denominator`

#### 14. Leverage & Debt Burden Metrics
- **Identifiers**: `total_debt`, `net_debt`, `debt_to_equity`, `debt_to_assets`, `interest_coverage`, `net_debt_to_ebitda`
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Net Debt} = \text{Total Debt} - \text{Cash}$
  - $\text{Debt-to-Equity} = \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Book Equity}}$
  - $\text{Debt-to-Assets} = \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Assets}}$
  - $\text{Interest Coverage} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Interest Expense}}$
  - $\text{Net Debt-to-EBITDA} = \frac{\text{Net Debt}}{\text{EBITDA}}$
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/liquidity-leverage/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_leverage_ratios`

### E. Operating Efficiency & Working Capital Cycles

#### 15. Turnover & Days Outstanding
- **Identifiers**: `inventory_turnover`, `days_inventory_outstanding`, `receivables_turnover`, `days_sales_outstanding`, `payables_turnover`, `days_payable_outstanding`, `cash_conversion_cycle`
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Inventory Turnover} = \frac{\text{COGS}}{\text{Average Inventory}}$
  - $\text{DIO} = \frac{\text{Average Inventory}}{\text{COGS}} \times \text{Days}$
  - $\text{Receivables Turnover} = \frac{\text{Net Revenue}}{\text{Average AR}}$
  - $\text{DSO} = \frac{\text{Average AR}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times \text{Days}$
  - $\text{Payables Turnover} = \frac{\text{COGS}}{\text{Average AP}}$
  - $\text{DPO} = \frac{\text{Average AP}}{\text{COGS}} \times \text{Days}$
  - $\text{Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)} = \text{DIO} + \text{DSO} - \text{DPO}$
- **Units**: Ratios ($\text{x}$) / Time ($\text{Days}$)
- **API Route**: `GET /api/v1/analytics/efficiency/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_operating_efficiency_and_cash_conversion_cycle`

### F. Contribution Margin & Break-Even Analysis

#### 16. Single-Product & Multi-Product Break-Even
- **Identifiers**: `contribution_margin`, `break_even_units`, `break_even_sales`, `margin_of_safety`
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Unit CM} = P - V$
  - $\text{CM Ratio} = \frac{\text{Unit CM}}{P} \times 100$
  - $\text{Break-Even Units} = \lceil \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Unit CM}} \rceil$
  - $\text{Break-Even Sales} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{CM Ratio}}$
  - $\text{Margin of Safety} = \text{Actual Sales} - \text{Break-Even Sales}$
  - $\text{Target Profit Volume} = \lceil \frac{\text{Fixed Costs} + \text{Target Profit}}{\text{Unit CM}} \rceil$
- **Multi-Product Sales Mix**:
  $$\text{Composite CM Ratio} = \frac{\sum (P_i - V_i) \cdot w_i}{\sum P_i \cdot w_i}$$
  $$\text{Composite Break-Even Sales} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Composite CM Ratio}}$$
- **API Route**:
  - `POST /api/v1/analytics/calculators/break-even/`
  - `POST /api/v1/analytics/calculators/break-even-multiproduct/`
- **Test Reference**: `tests/test_financial_metrics.py::TestFinancialCalculationEngine::test_single_and_multi_product_break_even`

## 3. Extending the Registry

To register a new metric:
1. Define the metric identifier, formula, unit, interpretation, and limitations in `analytics/services/metrics/registry.py`.
2. Implement the calculation logic inside the appropriate domain service (e.g. `profitability.py` or `liquidity.py`).
3. Return the standard `MetricResult` contract with complete status, inputs, and account provenance.
4. Add automated test coverage in `tests/test_financial_metrics.py`.
